I am a **Robotics & Physical AI Engineer at Freedom X**, the R&D lab of Freedom Holding, where I work on **embodied autonomy for last-mile delivery in urban environments**. 

### 🧭 What I work on

Urban sidewalks are among the hardest settings for a mobile robot: crowded, unstructured, and governed by mostly implicit rules. Our goal is a **reasoning-based policy for autonomous sidewalk navigation**, a single model that perceives the scene, reasons about it, and acts on it, understanding not only *what* to do but *why*.

### ✅ So far

- **Learned whole-body control:** reinforcement-learning policies for rough-terrain locomotion, goal-directed navigation and real-time obstacle avoidance, trained entirely in simulation.
- **Sim-to-real transfer:** deployed on the physical robot and running real deliveries in Almaty.
- **Photorealistic closed-loop simulation:** real recorded sidewalks reconstructed as neural 3D scenes with replayable pedestrians and vehicles.

### 🔭 Next

Moving from reactive control to **reasoning**: collecting real-world sidewalk data, scaling the simulator into a closed-loop training environment, and training a VLA policy that unifies perception, reasoning and action.
